# Course Editor: sources and evidence (researched October 2026)

Research:
- Guo, Kim and Rubin (2014), 6.9 million edX sessions: engagement by length and speaking rate. Summarised in Brame (2016), peer-reviewed: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5132380/ ; MIT News: https://news.mit.edu/2014/what-69-million-clicks-tell-us-about-how-fix-online-education
- Kim et al. (2014): 61% of interaction peaks align with visual transitions: https://up.csail.mit.edu/other-pubs/las2014-juhokim-peaks.pdf
- Mayer multimedia principles and effect sizes (contiguity largest): https://ugc.futurelearn.com/uploads/files/7d/d6/7dd6188d-c343-4311-b064-ac98d2c95abc/Multimedia_Principles._R._E._Mayer__2020.pdf
- Captions benefit comprehension (Gernsbacher 2015 review): https://successforkidswithhearingloss.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Video-Captions-Benefit-Everyone_2015.pdf

Platform rules:
- Udemy course and video standards (30 min, 5 lectures, 720p, 16:9, stereo): https://support.udemy.com/hc/articles/229232767-Video-Standards , https://business-support.udemy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022570274-Video-standards
- Skillshare quality and length: https://help.skillshare.com/hc/articles/205220897 , https://help.skillshare.com/hc/articles/4416700661517
- Caption line rules (DCMP via 3Play): https://www.3playmedia.com/blog/dcmp-closed-captioning-standards/

Notes: Teachable and Thinkific publish no per-video length; vendor "5 to 10 minutes" claims are unattributed. No platform publishes a course loudness target; -16 LUFS is borrowed from Apple's podcast spec as a defensible speech default. The 254 wpm "optimum" is a correlation; editing cannot create enthusiasm.
