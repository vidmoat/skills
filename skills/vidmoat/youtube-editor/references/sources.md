# YouTube Editor: sources and evidence (researched October 2026)

Primary:
- YouTube Help, key moments for audience retention (the first 30s "intro" metric): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415
- YouTube Help, end screens (last 5 to 20s, video at least 25s, up to 4 elements): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789
- YouTube Help, chapters (00:00, at least 3, each at least 10s): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579
- YouTube Help, recommended upload encoding (bitrates, H.264, 48 kHz AAC): https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171
- YouTube Help, Shorts up to 3 minutes for uploads from 15 Oct 2024: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877
- The MrBeast production memo ("How to succeed in MrBeast production"): first minute, 3:00 and 6:00 re-engagement, abrupt endings, integrated sponsor reads: https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/memo-how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/
- Walter Murch, Rule of Six (emotion 51%, story 23%, rhythm 10%): https://nofilmschool.com/2016/11/6-rules-good-cutting-according-oscar-winning-editor-walter-murch

Well established, not a YouTube rule:
- -14 LUFS normalisation (turned down only): https://productionadvice.co.uk/youtube-loudness/ ; -1 dBTP is common mastering practice.

Heuristics from vendor blogs (starting values, not evidence):
- "A visual change every 10 to 15s", "pattern interrupt at 25 to 35s", "intro under 5s": https://longstories.ai/blog/audience-retention-tips-long-form-videos
- Ali Abdaal style 2 to 5s talking-head shots: https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/ali-abdaal/

Disagreement: fast retention editing versus slower story. In March 2024 MrBeast said he slowed down and views rose (reported via the Washington Post, summarised at https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20240403-retention-editing-beastification-may-be-end). Structure (re-engagement, payoffs) is better supported than raw cut speed. "You need 50 to 70% retention" is a myth per Mario Joos (https://vidpros.com/retention-editing-what-you-can-learn-from-mario-joos/); compare against the channel's own typical retention.
