# UGC Ad Creator: sources and evidence (researched October 2026)

Primary (TikTok and regulators):
- Key message by 3s in over 63% of top-CTR auction ads (descriptive share, 2020), 120+ BPM tracks, vertical +25% 6s watch-through: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance
- 21 to 34s conversion window, 720p+, 9:16, text CTA, scene variety: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-that-drives-conversions
- 90% of ad-recall impact in the first 6s, CTA card recall, Kantar 88% sound: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads/
- FTC disclosures for influencers (in the video, audio and visual): https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers
- FTC final rule banning fake reviews and testimonials (August 2024): https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials

Secondary:
- TikTok Shop content styles (hook 3 to 6s tied to the product): https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-shoppable-video-content-guide-tells-creators-what-actually-works/
- Safe-zone measurements (no official fixed numbers from TikTok): https://www.poster.ly/tools/tiktok-safe-zone-checker ; Meta Reels 14/35/6%: https://blog.adnabu.com/meta-ads/meta-safe-zones/
- TikTok Shop misleading-claim guides: https://emplicit.co/avoiding-policy-violations-tiktok-shops/
- Hook and hold benchmarks (vendor): https://motionapp.com/blog/key-creative-performance-metrics

Folklore: a fixed "cut every 2 seconds" rate (the evidence is about scene count); "71% decide in 3 seconds" has no primary source; the 63% figure is often misquoted as "63% higher CTR".
