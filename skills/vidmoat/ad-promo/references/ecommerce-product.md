# E-commerce, unboxing and product ads

Read this for Shopify or marketplace product videos, unboxings and product-review ads.

E-commerce video answers three questions fast: what is it, why care, what now.

1. **First 2 s:** the product in USE, not on a shelf; a slow push-in on the hero shot (`punchIn` with a long `duration`, or scale keyframes).
2. **Feature beats:** 3 to 5 beats, each a close-up shot plus 3 to 5 words naming the benefit, 1.5 to 2.5 s each, landing on a cut.
3. **Detail shots move:** `applyMotionPreset` "slow-zoom" or "parallax-drift" on stills; scale or x keyframes to sweep across texture.
4. **Grade clean and bright:** product colour must look accurate. `setColor` saturation around 105 to 108 at most; no stylised look.
5. **Offer near the end**, using only the user's actual price and offer; the call to action in the final 1.5 to 2.5 s over footage.
6. **A stated duration is exact.**
