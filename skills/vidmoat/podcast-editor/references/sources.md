# Podcast Editor: sources and evidence (researched October 2026)

Primary:
- Apple Podcasts audio requirements: -16 LKFS plus or minus 1, true peak at or below -1 dBFS, two-channel files: https://podcasters.apple.com/support/audio-requirements
- Spotify podcast ad specs: -16 LUFS plus or minus 1.5, -2 dBTP: https://ads.spotify.com/en-GB/ad-specs/podcast-ad-specs/
- Spotify video podcast specs (1080p, about 25 Mbit/s, keyframes about every 1s): https://support.spotify.com/us/podcasters/article/video-specs/
- YouTube chapters and Shorts rules: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579 , https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877
- Mono vs stereo loudness: https://podnews.net/article/lufs-lkfs-for-podcasters

Craft convention (one production house, not measured): hold times, J/L offsets, minimum and maximum holds, punch-in size: https://www.finchley.co.uk/finchley-learning/video-engineering-in-a-professional-podcast-post-production-cutting-when-to-cut-and-why
Diary of a CEO uses 9 to 13 cameras and paces from drop-off curves (job post): https://www.entertainmentcareers.net/flightstory/long-form-video-editor-for-the-diary-of-a-ceo/job/520514/
Clip length and count: practitioner blogs (https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/podcast-youtube-shorts-strategy.html). "71% decide in 3 seconds" has no primary source. Spotify's own episode loudness spec is not public; "-14 for Spotify" is third-party.
