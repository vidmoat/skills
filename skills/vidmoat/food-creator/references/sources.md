# Food Creator: sources and evidence (researched October 2026)

- Finished dish first (consistent across creator guides, weak controlled evidence): https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/clip-cooking-recipes-food-content.html
- Tasty-style workflow, 2 to 4x speed-ups, about 30% of the procedure shown: https://blog.typito.com/working-backward-buzzfeeds-tasty-videos-produced-fast/ ; evolution of the format: https://www.ignitesocialmedia.com/video-marketing/evolution-tasty-video/
- Instagram Reels up to 3 minutes eligible for non-followers (January 2025): https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram
- Cooking video length on YouTube (3,859 videos, median 6.9 min, mean 10.4): https://ytgrowth.io/blog/video-length-by-niche
- Slow motion capture and shutter: https://nofilmschool.com/2017/04/complete-guide-lighting-shooting-and-editing-cooking-video
- White balance as the top creator mistake (Philip Lemoine): https://alphauniverse.com/stories/pro-tip-for-better-videos/
- Text timing (Netflix timed-text guidance): https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000239632-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-Supplementals-Marketing-Assets
- Colour on food (desaturate blue shadows; key greens separately): https://jonnyelwyn.co.uk/film-and-video-editing/cool-videos-films-projects-creative-work/pro-tips-to-improve-your-colour-grading/ , https://mixinglight.com/color-tutorial/color-correcting-food/
- Food sound layers and ASMR: https://sounddesign.irpr.agency/applications/food-videos/ , https://www.adobe.com/uk/creativecloud/video/discover/asmr.html
- One-action-per-shot trimming (Texas A&M AgriLife): https://dinnertonight.tamu.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2021/07/Videography-Tips-from-your-Editor.pdf

Disagreements: speed-ups suit short-form; long-form instructional guides keep natural speed. No primary source documents Joshua Weissman's or Ethan Chlebowski's cut rate.
