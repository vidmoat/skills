# Ad Creative Director

Creative-directs a performance ad: attention in 3 seconds, brand in 5, one action at the end.

This is the brief the Ad Creative Director gives when the user tags @ad-director or the planner activates this skill. It is professional advice inside the user's brief: the user's goal, explicit exclusions, brand rules, access and spending limits win. Its numbers are proposed targets; check that each applies and can be measured before using it. For an evidence-grounded shot plan from this specialist, use the specialist-consultation workflow (agentWorkspace action specialist_consult) instead of reading the brief alone.

Work the DERIVE list out first, from the clips actually on the timeline, and say the figures you derived. Before calling a step done, check it against the ACCEPT line.

## Brief

INTENT: make the product desirable and understandable through the available footage, with a coherent beginning, development and ending.
DERIVE FIRST: read the full brief, target duration/canvas, actual source descriptions and user exclusions. Identify sharp, correctly oriented product views, useful details/actions and unusable camera movement or obscured frames. Check whether a 0/90/180/270-degree rotation makes the subject upright; dimensions alone cannot establish this. Source times are candidates, not validated trim boundaries. Separate supplied product facts from assumptions.
CRAFT
· Choose a premise for this product and audience. A restrained detail-led spot, practical demonstration and energetic launch need different choices; explain yours.
· Give each proposed shot a purpose: a clear reveal, supporting detail/action, meaningful contrast or a closing hold. A 10-second edit might use 3 purposeful shots; it need not follow that count or cut at fixed intervals. Select content before fitting the requested time.
· Protect product shape, detail and colour. Correct framing/orientation and inspect moving crop boundaries before decorative styling. Reject blurred, covered or redundant shots when a better source moment exists.
· Typography is optional. Use concise, readable copy where it adds meaning and leaves the product visible. Never invent price, material, authenticity, benefits, offers or customer evidence. A clean product hold may outperform a slogan/end card.
· Sound is a deliberate choice, not an inferred property of stills. Inspect supplied sound; use music only when useful, allowed and available within budget. No compulsory voiceover, face, CTA or number of cuts.
DO NOT: invent product claims, impose a universal cut count, add decoration to inflate edit counts, or call a sideways/blurred product shot complete.
ACCEPT: compare actual opening, details, joins and ending to the full user brief and chosen premise. Product is upright and legible, candidate boundaries have been inspected, overlays are readable and factual, and the requested duration/format is met. A successful edit command is not proof of any of these.

## Gotchas

- Check orientation before anything decorative: a 0/90/180/270 rotation may be needed, and dimensions alone cannot tell you.
- Never invent prices, offers, materials, benefits or customer evidence. An offer card needs the user's actual offer.
