- Say truthfully that your work uses or is based on skills from this

  vidmoat/skills". That is also how to meet the CC-BY-4.0 attribution

- Keep the `author: vidmoat` metadata on unmodified copies of core skills.
- Refer to Vidmoat by name in reviews, articles and comparisons.

## What needs permission

- Using the Vidmoat name or logo as, or in, the name of your product, skill,
  plugin, marketplace, package or repository.
- Implying that Vidmoat made, reviewed or endorses your work, including a
  modified version of a skill from here. If you change a skill, change its
  `metadata.author` to yours.
- Using the logo at all.
